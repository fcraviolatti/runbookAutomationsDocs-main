# Project Calendars

You can also add calendars at project level. You will find a new menu on the project sidebar:

<figure>
<img src="https://docs.rundeck.com/docs/assets/img/calendars-project-home.png" alt="Project Calendars" />
<figcaption aria-hidden="true">Project Calendars</figcaption>
</figure>

When you click **Add Calendar**, the form has the same structure as the [System Calendars](https://docs.rundeck.com/docs/manual/calendars/system-calendars.html), the only difference is the calendar will be applied for all Jobs in a Project or for a selected list of jobs within the Project.

Now you will see a new section called **Jobs** where you can define where the calendar will be used. By default, it will not be assigned to any jobs.

<figure>
<img src="https://docs.rundeck.com/docs/assets/img/calendars-project-add-job-button.png" alt="Add a Job" />
<figcaption aria-hidden="true">Add a Job</figcaption>
</figure>

Click "Add a Job..."

<figure>
<img src="https://docs.rundeck.com/docs/assets/img/calendars-project-jobs-selector.png" alt="Job Detail" />
<figcaption aria-hidden="true">Job Detail</figcaption>
</figure>

Click on a Job name.

<figure>
<img src="https://docs.rundeck.com/docs/assets/img/calendars-project-jobs-selected.png" alt="Job Selected" />
<figcaption aria-hidden="true">Job Selected</figcaption>
</figure>

Also, you can remove jobs from the list clicking "Remove".

You can also check the "Apply this Calendar to All Jobs" checkbox, to apply to all Jobs in the project:

<figure>
<img src="https://docs.rundeck.com/docs/assets/img/calendars-project-jobs.png" alt="All Jobs" />
<figcaption aria-hidden="true">All Jobs</figcaption>
</figure>

Save the Calendar.

Then, in the Jobs page you will see the list of calendars applied to the Job's schedule:

<figure>
<img src="https://docs.rundeck.com/docs/assets/img/calendars-project-jobs-list.png" alt="Job List" />
<figcaption aria-hidden="true">Job List</figcaption>
</figure>
